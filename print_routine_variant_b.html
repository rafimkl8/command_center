⚡ Why this mode wins

  • Post-Fajr = silent, zero interruptions
  • Your sharpest 2 hours hit the hardest topic
  • Midday nap restores afternoon focus
  • Evening stays light → protects family + sleep

🚫 Non-negotiables

  • Wake = Fajr, same time daily (no snooze)
  • The midday nap is mandatory here — don't skip it
  • Screens off by 22:00
  • No caffeine after Asr (~4:30 pm)
  • Gym 6×/week (Sat–Thu) · Friday rest
  • 1 Git commit every single day
  • If you slept badly → fall back to Variant A that day

✅ Today is a win if…
